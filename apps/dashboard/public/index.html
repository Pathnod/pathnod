What this dashboard can tell you

Observations and confidence scores help assess a device, but do not prove its exact GPS location or that each observer is a different person. A reward allocation is not a completed payment. This dashboard lets you browse public records; it cannot change them or move funds.

Understand the limitations

A nearby exchange may still be relayed by an attacker. Raw observations and grouped witnesses are different measures. Confidence reflects the evidence available at the report's evaluation time, not continuous monitoring.
